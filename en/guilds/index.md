This section covers settings that belong to a guild as a whole rather than to one channel,
member or message. You're assumed to have a bot named `"my-bot"` logged in (see
[Starting a bot](doc:getting-started/starting-a-bot)) and to know
[cache and await](doc:concepts/cache-and-await).

## Reading order

1. [Welcome screen](welcome-screen.md): the screen a community guild shows to new members,
   read and edited from a script.

Rules that filter what members post are covered in the [AutoMod section](doc:automod).
